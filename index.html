Before you arrive

What are the hours of operation?

Friday & Saturday: 4:00 PM – 2:00 AM.
Sunday: 2:00 PM – 8:00 PM.

What is the dress code?

Dress to impress. Come sharp, come stylish — upscale nightlife attire is the vibe at PADI.

How do I make a reservation?

Call or text us at 631-703-1601 or +1 (563) 607-7032 to reserve a VIP table or plan your celebration.

Where are you located?

214 Walnut St, Muscatine, Iowa 52761 — right in the heart of downtown.

What kind of music do you play?

Afrobeat, Reggaeton, and R&B — with Afrobeat Fridays, Reggaeton Saturdays, and our R&B Sunday day party.

Do I need ID?

Yes — a valid government-issued photo ID or passport is required for entry.
